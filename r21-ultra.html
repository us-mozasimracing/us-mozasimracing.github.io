Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the MOZA R21 Ultra DD Wheel Base

Is the R21 Ultra’s 21Nm torque sustained or peak output? What does that mean for me? +
The R21 Ultra’s 21Nm rating refers to sustained torque, not a short-term peak. This means the base can maintain full performance even during long, demanding endurance sessions without torque throttling—ensuring consistent, realistic feedback every lap.
How does 21Nm compare to other wheel bases on the market? Who is it for? +
21Nm sits in the upper mid-range of direct drive wheel bases—stronger than most entry and mid-tier models (typically 3–17Nm) yet more accessible than extreme flagship units. It’s ideal for advanced sim racers or semi-professional drivers who demand sustained torque, precision, and smooth control for competitive racing.
What’s the difference between the R21 and previous MOZA bases like the R5 or R9? Why should I upgrade? +
The R21 Ultra introduces major upgrades:
  • Higher sustained torque at 21Nm for endurance performance;
  • Next-gen zero-cogging flat-wire motor and 21-bit magnetic encoder for ultra-smooth precision;
  • Intelligent cooling for stable thermal efficiency;
  • Advanced force feedback algorithms and customizable app tuning for personalized feel and control.
How is thermal performance and long-term durability? Are there safety features? +
The R21 features intelligent thermal management with real-time temperature monitoring and torque regulation to maintain optimal performance. A built-in Hands-off Protection system automatically centers and stabilizes the wheel if your hands leave it—enhancing safety during use.
Do the RGB lighting and app features affect performance? Can I turn them off? +
No. The RGB lighting is purely aesthetic and telemetry-driven—it can be fully customized or turned off. The MOZA App handles configuration and firmware updates only; all critical force feedback processing is handled locally on the high-speed microcontroller for zero performance loss.
