Precision Racing Wheel for Ultimate Sim Control

Hand-stitched eco-friendly microfiber leather, a fully customizable 2.99-inch high-definition display, and magnetic forged carbon paddle shifters with Hall sensors. Every button, paddle, and LED is designed for real-time control, delivering a fully immersive cockpit experience.

Hand-Stitched Microfiber Leather Grip

Wrapped in eco-friendly microfiber leather with seamless stitching at the thumb rests, the grip offers a secure, comfortable feel while maintaining durability for extended racing sessions.

Customizable 2.99-inch HD Display

The integrated 2.99-inch high-definition screen supports fully customizable dashboards through MOZA Pit House, allowing drivers to create, import, and share UI layouts tailored to different cars and racing disciplines.

Carbon Fiber Reinforced Construction

Built with carbon fiber-reinforced composite front and back plates paired with an aluminum alloy frame, the CS Pro delivers high torsional rigidity and reduced inertia for precise, direct force feedback response.

Fully Customizable RGB Lighting System

Ten RGB rev lights, six RGB flag lights, and four 12-position RGB rotary switches provide real-time telemetry feedback and visual cues, all fully adjustable with up to 16.7 million color combinations.

Magnetic Forged Carbon Dual-Clutch Paddles

Motorsport-inspired forged carbon paddles with Hall sensors deliver precise, consistent actuation. The dual-clutch system supports multiple modes, including dual-clutch launch, single clutch, or fully programmable inputs.

Expandable 6-Paddle Configuration

Designed for future expansion, the CS Pro supports an optional 6-paddle layout with pre-installed mounting points and connectors, enabling advanced control for DRS, KERS, or vehicle adjustments.

Extensive & Fully Programmable Inputs

With 8 RGB buttons, 4 twelve-position rotary encoders, 2 seven-way funky switches, 2 thumbwheels, and rear buttons, every control is fully customizable to suit complex race strategies.

Open Ecosystem & Broad Compatibility

Compatible with all MOZA wheelbases and supporting direct PC connection via the MOZA hub kit, the CS Pro also works with third-party wheelbases—built for flexible, open sim racing setups.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the MOZA CS Pro Steering Wheel

Who is the CS Pro best suited for? +
The CS Pro is an ultra-versatile 325 mm standard diameter wheel engineered for drivers who race GT3, touring cars, rally, drifting, NASCAR, and casual cruising. Its authentic circular rim format, high-torque rigidity, and extensive rotary dials make it the ultimate all-around wheel.
Is a 325 mm round wheel suitable for competitive racing? +
Absolutely. Real-world GT and rally cars use 320–330 mm diameter wheels for optimal leverage and precise counter-steering. The lightweight carbon composite construction keeps rotational inertia low, ensuring lightning-fast force feedback transmission from direct drive wheelbases.
Is the wheel rim interchangeable? +
The CS Pro features an integrated, high-rigidity structural design with front and rear carbon composite plates and aerospace aluminum frame to ensure zero creaking or flex under high-torque direct drive conditions.
Can I customize the display and dashboards? +
Yes! Through MOZA Pit House, you have full control over the 2.99-inch color telemetry screen. Choose from dozens of motorsport dashboard presets or customize telemetry channels like speed, gear, RPM, lap delta, tire temps/pressures, and fuel level.
What are the advantages of the dual-clutch paddle system? +
The dual-clutch paddles utilize non-contact Hall effect magnetic sensors for millimeter-level precision. In race starts, you can set an exact clutch bite-point: hold both paddles, drop one at the green light, and smoothly release the second to launch without wheelspin.
